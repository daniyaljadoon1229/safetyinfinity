Safety Infinity

Every hazard. Your whole HSE toolkit.

Standards, clauses, 98 document templates, permits, PPE, investigations, exam practice, your own private workspace, and a company HSE workspace with dashboards, KPIs, reports, certificates and team alerts, in English or Arabic, for HSE officers, engineers, managers and their teams in oil and gas, construction, facility management, hospitality, petrochemicals, offshore, production, healthcare and aviation.

What's inside

  • Library: 239 standards with clause references (OSHA, NFPA, ISO, IEC, UK HSE, EU, ILO, API, ASME, IOGP, IMO, ICAO, ANSI/ISEA, Australia, Canada, the GCC and more), a register of 75 hazards and step-by-step site guides. Any page can be read aloud by a female voice in English, Arabic, Urdu or Hindi.
  • Saudi Arabia: Labor Law, Civil Defense, SBC 801, CBAHI, SAIS, GACA, Balady, NRRC, Saudi Aramco (CSM, GIs, MMSR) and SABIC.
  • Company HSE workspace: projects, weekly and monthly HSE reports with manpower and man-hours, and registers for incidents (fatality, LTI, RWC, MTC, first aid, near miss and more), NCRs with close-out, trainings, inductions, drills, audits and equipment certificates. A dashboard with safe man-hours, LTIFR, TRIR, the incident pyramid and leading indicators shows trends from 1 month to 5 years, a KPI scorecard tracks your targets, and monthly, weekly and KPI reports build themselves. Training certificates and employee of the month certificates, notice-board posters in four languages, and incident and NCR alerts with a different sound for each level. Share one workspace with your team as admin, editor or viewer.
  • 98 document templates: HSE plan and policy, HIRA, risk assessments, JHA, method statements, COSHH, lifting and rescue plans, flash reports, investigations and RCA, inspections, permits, emergency plans, drill reports, NCRs, meeting minutes and more. Fill them in on your phone, attach photos, PDF and Word files, and print them or save them as PDF with your letterhead.
  • Toolkit: 5×5 risk matrix and assessment methods, permit types and lifecycle, 19 daily checklists, incident investigation techniques, ISO 45001 and ISO 14001 clause guides, HSE document suite, role guides and KPI calculators.
  • PPE guide: pictures of each item and what to wear for 57 activities, part by part, with the US and European standard for every item, plus selection tables: welding shades, respirator protection factors, filter codes, glove and footwear markings, hi-vis classes, arc flash categories and fall arrest limits.
  • Learn: certification pathways (NEBOSH, IOSH, BCSP, IIRSM, OTHM, ISEP, BICS, IRATA and more), 15 original practice papers and 6 animated site scenarios.
  • My Work: create risk assessments, JSAs, permits, inspections, investigations, observations, toolbox talks and certificate records; export reports and backups. Your work is private to your account and syncs to any phone you sign in to.
  • Your way: English or Arabic, six colour themes, Auto, Day or Night mode, and your choice of sounds.
  • Your account: sign in with your username, email or mobile number. Mobile numbers from every country are supported.

Sources and independence

Safety Infinity summarizes and references standards and regulations published by their owners, including government bodies such as OSHA (osha.gov), the UK HSE (hse.gov.uk), Saudi Arabia's Ministry of Human Resources and Social Development (hrsd.gov.sa), the General Directorate of Civil Defense (998.gov.sa) and the Saudi Building Code National Committee (sbc.gov.sa). Each entry in the app links to its official source.

Safety Infinity is independent. It does not represent any government, regulator, standards body, awarding body or company, and is not endorsed by them. Practice papers are original questions, not past papers.
