Delete your Safety Infinity account

App: Safety Infinity · Developer: Daniyal Khalid

In the app (fastest)

  1. Open Safety Infinity and sign in.
  2. Tap the gear icon at the top right to open Settings, then About Safety Infinity (or open My Work).
  3. Under Account, tap Delete account, then tap again to confirm.

Your account is deleted straight away.

By email

If you can't sign in, email YOUR-EMAIL@example.com from the email address on your account with the subject "Delete my Safety Infinity account" and your username. We delete the account within 30 days and confirm by email.

What is deleted

  • Your username, email address, mobile number and sign-in details.
  • All My Work records and their attachments stored in your account (export a backup first if you want to keep them).
  • The phones registered for alerts and your read-aloud usage counter.
  • Your membership of any company team workspace. A workspace you are the only member of is deleted with all its records. In a shared workspace, records you added stay for the company, and if you were its only admin another member becomes admin.
  • On request, your subscription record at RevenueCat.

Purchase records stay with Apple or Google as their rules require. Deleting your account does not cancel your subscription. Cancel it in your App Store or Google Play account settings to stop renewals.
