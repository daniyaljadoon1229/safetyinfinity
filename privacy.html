Safety Infinity

Privacy policy

Effective 19 October 2026 · App: Safety Infinity 4.0 · Developer: Daniyal Khalid

Summary

Safety Infinity has no advertising, no analytics and no tracking. To use the app you create a Safety Infinity account with a username, email address and mobile number. We use these only to run your account, keep it secure, sync your work and, if you turn them on, send your team's safety alerts. Payments are handled by Apple or Google, and your subscription status by RevenueCat.

Your account

When you create an account we collect your username, email address, mobile number and a password. Your password is stored only as a secure hash by Firebase Authentication; we never see it. We send a link to your email and a code by SMS to your mobile number to confirm they are yours. We use your username, email or number to let you sign in, to recover your account and to contact you about it. We do not use them for marketing and do not sell or share them for advertising.

Accounts run on Google Firebase (Firebase Authentication, Cloud Firestore and Cloud Functions), operated by Google LLC as our processor under the Firebase data processing terms. SMS codes are sent by Google to the number you enter, from any country.

Your work

Risk assessments, permits, inspections, investigations, observations, exam results, documents, notes and other records you create in My Work are stored privately in your account in Cloud Firestore, so they are available on any phone you sign in to. Only you can read them; security rules block everyone else, including other users. A copy is kept on your phone for offline use and removed when you sign out. Files you export, print or share go only where you send them.

Attachments

You can attach photos, PDF, Word and other files to records and documents. They are stored with the record they belong to: privately in your account for My Work, or in the team workspace for company records, where every member of that workspace can open them. Photos are reduced in size before upload. The app asks for access to your camera or photos only when you choose to attach a picture, and reads only the pictures and files you pick.

Company team workspaces

If you create or join a company workspace, the records in it (projects, weekly and monthly reports, incidents, NCRs, trainings, inductions, drills, audits, equipment certificates, employee of the month records, notice-board posters, KPI targets, attachments and the company profile, including any names and details you or your colleagues type into them) are stored in Cloud Firestore and can be read by every member of that workspace. The workspace admin decides who is a member and whether they can change records (admin or editor) or only read them (viewer). Members can see each other's usernames and roles, and records show the username of the person who last changed them. Your email address and mobile number are never shown to other members. Enter personal or medical details of other people only where your company has a lawful reason to record them, and only as much as needed. When you leave a workspace or delete your account you lose access to it; records you added stay in the workspace for the company unless an admin deletes them. Deleting a workspace deletes all of its records.

Team alerts (push notifications)

Alerts are optional. If you turn them on in Settings → Notifications, the app stores in your account a push token for that phone (a random identifier issued by Apple or Google), whether it is an iPhone or Android phone, its language, which alerts you want and the sound you chose for each level. When a member of your team workspace reports an incident or an NCR, or changes an NCR's status, our server sends an alert to the other members' phones through Firebase Cloud Messaging (Google) and, for iPhones, the Apple Push Notification service. An alert contains the type of event, the record's title and number, the workspace name and the username of the person who reported it. We use push tokens only to deliver these alerts, never for advertising. Tokens are removed when a phone stops accepting alerts, when you turn alerts off and when you delete your account.

Read aloud and translation

With the natural voice, the text of the library page you choose to hear is sent from our server to Google Cloud Text-to-Speech, which returns the audio. To hear a page in Arabic, Urdu or Hindi, its text is first sent to Google Cloud Translation. Only library text is sent, not your records. Translations of library text are kept in a shared cache so the same page isn't translated twice; the cache contains no personal information. We keep a monthly count of characters used by your account to apply the fair-use allowance. Google processes this text as our processor under the Google Cloud data processing terms. With the phone's voice option, text is read by your phone's own speech engine and isn't sent to us.

Your language, theme, day or night mode and sound choices are stored only on your phone.

Subscriptions and payments

You buy Safety Infinity Pro through the Apple App Store or Google Play. Apple or Google handle payment; we never see your card or bank details. Their privacy policies apply to the purchase.

To check whether your subscription is active, the app uses RevenueCat (RevenueCat, Inc.), a subscription management service. RevenueCat receives your account's random user ID (not your name, email or phone number), your purchase history for Safety Infinity (product, price, currency, store country, purchase and renewal dates, store receipt or token), and technical data needed to answer the request, such as app version, operating system and IP address. We use this only to unlock the app, manage your subscription and see total subscription numbers. RevenueCat processes this data for us under its privacy policy.

Keeping and deleting your data

We keep your account and records until you delete your account. You can delete it at any time in the app under Settings → About Safety Infinity → Account → Delete account, or by following the steps on our account deletion page. Deleting your account permanently removes your username, email address, mobile number, sign-in details, My Work records and their attachments, alert tokens and usage counters within minutes, and removes you from any team workspace (a workspace you are the only member of is deleted with its records). Your purchase records stay with Apple or Google; we delete the subscription record at RevenueCat on request. Deleting your account does not cancel a subscription: cancel it in your App Store or Google Play settings.

Security

Data is encrypted in transit (HTTPS/TLS) and at rest by Google Cloud. Access to your records is limited to your signed-in account after your email and mobile number are verified.

Internet use

The library works offline once you are signed in. When a connection is available, the app downloads a public file listing recent changes to standards (updates.json) from GitHub Pages. This request contains no personal information. Like any web server, GitHub may process technical data such as your IP address under the GitHub Privacy Statement.

Links to official sources open in your browser. Those websites have their own privacy policies.

Children

Safety Infinity is a professional tool and is not directed at children under 16. We do not knowingly collect information from children.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, including under Saudi Arabia's Personal Data Protection Law and the EU GDPR, you may have the right to access, correct, export or delete your personal data and to object to processing. You can export your records in the app (My Work → Export backup) and delete your account in the app. For anything else, email us and we will respond within 30 days. Your data may be processed in data centres outside your country by Google, Apple (for iPhone alerts) and RevenueCat under appropriate safeguards.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date. Questions or requests: YOUR-EMAIL@example.com.
